HIGHER-ORDER PROCESS DYNAMICS: Because the controller only measures and acts upon Tank 2 (h2), Tank 1 acts as an unmeasured intermediate lag. When tuning gains optimized for an instantaneous single-tank model (Kp=2.1, Ki=0.35) are applied here, Tank 1 head surges dramatically before Tank 2 reaches setpoint, producing over 90% overshoot and prolonged settling time.

PHYSICAL PROCESS ASYMMETRY: The heating element cannot refrigerate or actively cool the process ($P_{heater} \ge 0$). If excessive $K_p$ or $K_i$ overshoots the setpoint, the heater immediately shuts off ($0\%$), and the chamber cools strictly through Newton's law of cooling: $\dot{Q}_{loss} = (T - T_{amb}) \cdot h_{loss}$. Notice how the downward slope after an overshoot peak is significantly slower and flatter than the rapid upward heating ramp!
